Chipload Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Chipload is a machinist calculator for Android and the web, made by Brennan Meyer. The short version: Chipload does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Everything you type — calculator inputs, history, favorites, machine profiles, tool library, saved jobs, and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your phone unless you choose to share a result or export a file yourself.

One thing Android itself may do: if backup is turned on for your Google account, Android keeps a private copy of the app's data with your other phone backups, so your machines, tools, and saved jobs come back on a new phone. That copy belongs to your Google account. Chipload never sees it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. No sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No server of ours. The app works fully offline.
  • No permissions beyond what Android grants every app (internet access is used only by Google Play for purchases).

Purchases

The one-time "Pro" unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Chipload only learns whether your Google account owns the unlock, and caches that answer on the device so Pro keeps working offline. We never see your payment details.

Sharing and exports

If you tap Share, Save CSV/DXF, or Print, the data goes wherever you send it (another app, a file, a printer). Chipload does not keep a copy.

Children

Chipload is a general-audience tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.
